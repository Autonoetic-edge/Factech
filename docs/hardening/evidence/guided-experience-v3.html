    Your privacy choices

    This preview uses a sample account and stores nothing. Face-template permission and optional evaluation recording are separate choices.

    Face template

    A numerical representation of your face is linked to your own account for future face checks. “Agree & continue” grants this permission in the demo. Production must display the current retention and deletion policy before collecting consent.

    Optional evaluation recording

    Authorized reviewers may inspect a saved recording to evaluate the system. Proposed retention is up to seven days. Withdrawal and deletion must be available through your own account before rollout.

    Test recording is off.

    One check. Three stages.

    1. Prepare. Review your privacy choices and allow camera access when ready.
    2. Follow the guide. Face forward, then make each small head turn when prompted. Keep both eyes visible.
    3. Get your result. The camera turns off after capture. You will see the outcome and one next action.

    You can stop before submission. If the connection drops after submission, check the existing result before starting again.
