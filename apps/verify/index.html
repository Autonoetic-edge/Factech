Your privacy choices

Face template

When you agree, a numerical representation of your face is saved, encrypted, against your own account and used only to compare with your later face checks. It is not a photo and cannot be viewed as one.

Camera images

Images from your camera are sent once, over a secure connection, to make the decision. This page does not ask to keep a recording, so none is stored.

Your choices

You can stop before anything is submitted. You can withdraw your agreement and have your template deleted; ask your workspace administrator.

One check. Three stages.

  1. Prepare. Review your privacy choices and allow camera access when ready.
  2. Follow the guide. Face forward, then make each small head turn when prompted. Keep both eyes visible.
  3. Get your result. The camera turns off after capture. You will see the outcome and one next action.

You can stop before submission. If the connection drops after submission, check the existing result before starting again.

The small head figure is guidance only. The decision is made by the server, never by this page.
